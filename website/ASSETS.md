# Demo asset provenance

`public/demo/synthetic-source.webp` depicts a fictional adult generated with
OpenAI ImageGen on 25 August 2026. It does not represent a real person.

The prompt requested a photorealistic, front-facing everyday portrait with
natural skin texture, clear eyes and shoulders, mixed indoor light, and a busy
home-office background. It explicitly excluded known people, logos, text,
watermarks, studio lighting, and beauty retouching.

`public/demo/biyoves-print-sheet.webp` is the real output produced from that
source by BiyoVes 1.4.0 using the biometric 50 × 60 mm standard, four-photo
layout, white background, and 300 DPI export. Preflight results were: blur
score 105.7, eyes open, and face angle 0.2 degrees.
